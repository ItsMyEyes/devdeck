A fresh DevDeck instance has no accounts yet, so the first thing you'll see
is a login screen with nowhere to go but **Register**.

## Register

Enter an email and a password (12 characters minimum). What happens next
depends on whether **2FA** is turned on for the instance you're connecting
to — 2FA (two-factor authentication) is an extra "prove it's really you"
step using an app on your phone, on top of your password:

- **2FA off** — you're logged in right away, no extra steps.
- **2FA on** (the default for hub/runtime builds) — you're sent to a **set
  up 2FA** screen:
  1. Open an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Authy,
     1Password, and similar all work) and scan the QR code shown.
  2. Type the 6-digit code the app generates, to confirm it worked.
  3. Save the one-time **backup codes** shown on screen — see below.

<Callout title="Save your backup codes" type="warn">
  You won't see these codes again after this screen. They're your only way
  back in if you ever lose your phone or your authenticator app — without
  them, nobody can self-service recover a locked-out account.
</Callout>

From then on, logging in is a two-step form: email and password first, then
the 6-digit code (or one of your backup codes if you don't have the app on
hand).

## Where you land

Either way, you land on the home screen. Since you don't have a workspace
yet, it shows an onboarding screen prompting you to create one — continue to
[Workspaces and projects](/docs/getting-started/workspaces-and-projects).
